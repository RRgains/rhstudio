Our Story

A Culinary Journey by the Sea

Founded in 1983, by our grandparents and great-grandparents, Kaira sushi has been serving authentic Japanese cuisine with a modern twist. Our passion for fresh, local ingredients and traditional recipes has made us a beloved destination for both locals and visitors.

Every dish tells a story of our connection to the ocean and the rich culinary heritage of our region. Our Chef combines time-honored techniques with innovative approaches to create unforgettable dining experiences.

A Hidden Gem

Praia do Coelha is one of the most beautiful and unspoiled beaches in the region. With its golden sands, crystal-clear waters, and dramatic cliffs, it offers a perfect escape from the hustle and bustle of everyday life.

The beach is known for its tranquil atmosphere and stunning natural beauty. It's the perfect place to relax before or after your meal at our restaurant.

We're proud to be part of this special place and to share its beauty with our guests.
